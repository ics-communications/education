## Blocking — do not send to press until these are answered

### 1. MA-EL net cost — the printed figure is unreachable from the booklet's own numbers

**The booklet currently prints:** `~$17,000` (P5, "Net cost")

**Why it doesn't reconcile.** Page 7 states a course is `$1,500 · 3 credit hours` at `$500 per credit hour`. Pages 3 and 5 state MA-EL is `10 courses`. The site elsewhere states 45 credit hours. Those inputs give three different answers, and none of them is $17,000:

| Reading | Gross | Net of $500/semester bursary |
|---|---|---|
| 10 courses × $1,500 | $15,000 | ~$12,000 |
| 45 credit hours × $500 | $22,500 | ~$17,500–$19,500 depending on semester count |
| **Printed in the booklet** | — | **~$17,000** |

**The MWS-E figures reconcile exactly**, which is what isolates MA-EL as the error rather than the fee model:
6 × $1,500 − $2,000 = **$7,000** · 7 × $1,500 − $2,000 = **$8,500** — precisely the printed `$7,000–$8,500` range.

**What's needed:** one authoritative net figure, stated to the same precision as MWS-E (a real number, not `~$17K`).

**Who can answer:** Academic Registrar / Finance.

### 2. MA-EL credit hours — 30 or 45?

**The booklet currently prints:** `10 courses` (P4 and P5), with no credit-hour number.

The credit-hour figure was **removed** from P5 so the page would stop contradicting itself — 10 courses × 3 credit hours is 30, not the 45 the site publishes. Removal made the page self-consistent; it did not establish the truth.

**What's needed:** confirm whether an MA-EL course is 3 or 4.5 credit hours. If ICS wants a credit-hour number in print at all, it has to survive multiplication by the course count on the facing page. If the answer is messy, the safest print position is course counts only, with credit hours left to the website — which is what the build does.

**Who can answer:** Academic Registrar / Registrar's Office.

### 3. MWS-E capstone — is the seventh course the capstone, or additional?

**The booklet currently prints two statements that cannot both be true:**

- P3 / P4 stat row: `6–7 courses` … `Action Project + paper, or major research paper`
- P4 distinctive: *"**Two ways to finish** — the Action Project, or a seventh course and a major research paper."*

If the research-paper pathway requires a seventh course *in addition* to six, then "6–7 courses" is right but the capstone column is misleading. If the seventh course *is* the capstone, the distinctive is misleading. A prospective student comparing cost and duration will do this arithmetic.

**Note:** the length figure moved to **~2.5 years** in this pass on the reasoning that six courses is two years and the project-plus-paper is the half. That reasoning implies the capstone is *additional* to the six — but it was given as a duration estimate, not as a structural ruling, so this item stands.

**Who can answer:** Program Director (Dr. van der Boom).

## Confirm before press — high value, not blocking

### 4. Is there anything a student must *do* to get the $500 bursary?

The build now says **"(as available)"** everywhere the bursary appears, and the centre-spread stat note reads *"Net cost assumes the $500 semester bursary, as available"* — because it is donor-funded and cannot be guaranteed.

That covers the *availability* question. It does not cover the *mechanism*: does an eligible student receive it automatically, or apply for it? One clause settles it — "applied automatically each semester" or "apply once at registration". A reader who assumes the wrong one feels misled at the invoice.

**Who can answer:** Academic Registrar / Finance.

## Assets required from ICS

### 12. A portrait cover photograph with a woman in it — the current one prints at 156 dpi

`img/seminar-table.jpg` is 2048 × 1365 px, a 3:2 **landscape** image on a 5.75 × 8.75 in **portrait** artboard. Under `background-size: cover` the crop is height-driven: 1365 px spanning 8.75 in = **156 dpi**, or 52% of the 300 dpi print spec, on the largest and most scrutinised surface in the piece. Everything else is healthy — headshots run 1,122–1,504 dpi, logos 529–581 dpi, QR codes 625–715 dpi.

Confirmed in the review call as a placeholder, with three requirements:

- **A woman in the frame.** Most ICS education students are women; the current cover is two men.
- **A conversation, not a posed shot** — the argument of the piece is a community of practice.
- **≥ 2625 px tall**, or a portrait crop from a ≥ 2625 px original.

Also on the current image: a legible third-party trademark (a Patagonia cap logo) on the subject.

**Known candidate:** interview stills of Carolyn Bentum exist, but on a purple background that fights the cover treatment. Retreat photography is the other source — and the education cohort's attendance at the retreat is itself thin, which is worth solving for reasons beyond this cover.
