# Open Questions — Educational Leadership Booklet

**Piece:** 8pp saddle-stitched recruitment booklet, 5.5 × 8.5 in
**Build:** `edu-booklet/index.html` (authoritative) · **Copy:** `edu-booklet/copy-deck.md` (transcribed from the build)
**Status:** design and copy are print-ready. The items below are **not** design decisions — they are facts only ICS can confirm, plus two assets that must be supplied.

Print is unforgiving: every one of these is cheap to fix now and expensive to fix after a press run. Items 1–3 are blocking.

---

## Blocking — do not send to press until these are answered

### 1. MA-EL net cost — the printed figure is unreachable from the booklet's own numbers

**The booklet currently prints:** `~$17,000` (P5, "Net cost")

**Why it doesn't reconcile.** Page 7 states a course is `$1,500 · 3 credit hours` at `$500 per credit hour`. Pages 3 and 5 state MA-EL is `10 courses`. The site elsewhere states 45 credit hours. Those inputs give three different answers, and none of them is $17,000:

| Reading | Gross | Net of $500/semester bursary |
|---|---|---|
| 10 courses × $1,500 | $15,000 | ~$12,000 |
| 45 credit hours × $500 | $22,500 | ~$17,500–$19,500 depending on semester count |
| **Printed in the booklet** | — | **~$17,000** |

**The MWS-E figures reconcile exactly**, which is what isolates MA-EL as the error rather than the fee model:
6 × $1,500 − $2,000 = **$7,000** · 7 × $1,500 − $2,000 = **$8,500** — precisely the printed `$7,000–$8,500` range.

**What's needed:** one authoritative net figure, stated to the same precision as MWS-E (a real number, not `~$17K`). This was flagged as PLAN §6 items 1–3 before layout began; each was answered individually but never reconciled against the others or against the P7 fee grid.

**Who can answer:** Academic Registrar / Finance.

---

### 2. MA-EL credit hours — 30 or 45?

**The booklet currently prints:** `10 courses` (P4 and P5), with no credit-hour number.

The credit-hour figure was **removed** from P5 during the distill so the page would stop contradicting itself — 10 courses × 3 credit hours is 30, not the 45 the site publishes. Removal made the page self-consistent; it did not establish the truth.

**What's needed:** confirm whether an MA-EL course is 3 or 4.5 credit hours. If ICS wants a credit-hour number in print at all, it has to survive multiplication by the course count on the facing page. If the answer is messy, the safest print position is to keep course counts only and leave credit hours to the website — which is what the build currently does.

**Who can answer:** Academic Registrar / Registrar's Office.

---

### 3. MWS-E capstone — is the seventh course the capstone, or additional?

**The booklet currently prints two statements that cannot both be true:**

- P3 / P4 stat row: `6–7 courses` … `Action Project + paper, or major research paper`
- P4 distinctive: *"**Two ways to finish** — the Action Project, or a seventh course and a major research paper."*

If the research-paper pathway requires a seventh course *in addition* to six, then "6–7 courses" is right but the capstone column is misleading. If the seventh course *is* the capstone, the distinctive is misleading. A prospective student comparing cost and duration will do this arithmetic.

**Who can answer:** Program Director (Dr. van der Boom).

## Confirm before press — high value, not blocking

### 4. Apr 1 / Aug 1 / Dec 1 — deadlines or start dates?

**The booklet used to print the same three dates under two contradictory labels:**

- P4 / P5 stat row: `Start dates` — Apr 1 · Aug 1 · Dec 1
- P7: `Application Deadlines` — Apr 1 (Summer) · Aug 1 (Fall) · Dec 1 (Winter)

Apr 1 cannot be both the day a Summer course begins and the day the Summer application is due. P7's reading is the better-supported one — a Fall term does not begin on Aug 1, and a Winter term does not begin on Dec 1, so these look like deadlines roughly a month ahead of each start.

**What the build does now:** the dates appear **once**, on P7, under the deadline label. The centre-spread rows were reduced to `Starts — Three per year` and `Starts — Two per year`, which is true under either reading. Nothing was asserted; the contradiction was removed by not repeating the dates.

**What's needed:** confirm P7's label is right. If these are in fact start dates, P7's heading is the string that has to change.

**Also on that grid:** `MA-EL — Apr 1 (Summer or Fall) · Nov 1 (Winter or Summer)` lists Summer against both dates. A reader cannot tell which Summer each refers to.

**Who can answer:** Academic Registrar.

---

### 5. Is the $500 bursary automatic?

The booklet names the bursary five times and every net-cost figure assumes it, but never says whether a student receives it automatically or has to apply.

If it is automatic, saying so is worth more than any other sentence on P7 — it converts a discount into a price. If it has conditions, a reader who assumes otherwise will feel misled at the invoice.

**What's needed:** one clause, e.g. "applied automatically each semester" or "apply once at registration".

**Who can answer:** Academic Registrar / Finance.

---

### 6. QECO / OCT pay-grid recognition

**The booklet says nothing about this, and neither does the site.**

For a working Canadian teacher weighing $8,500–$17,000 over two to three years, what the degree does to their salary category is *the* practical question. One approved sentence on P7 would be worth more to a browsing teacher than all of page 2.

**What's needed:** either an approved, accurate line — or an explicit decision to stay silent. **Do not guess in print.** If ICS cannot state a category outcome, saying nothing is correct.

**Who can answer:** Academic Registrar, possibly in consultation with QECO/OCT.

---

### 7. A named human for the call to action

**The booklet currently routes every reader to "the Academic Registrar"** — an office, named five times, with no person attached.

The piece promises *"faculty who know your name"* and cohorts of twelve. Routing every enquiry to an anonymous office contradicts that promise in a way readers feel without articulating. Print collateral converts better with a person: a name, a direct line, and a headshot sized like the faculty photos on P7.

**What's needed:** a name and direct contact, plus permission to print them. If the role turns over often, a role-based alias with a first name ("Ask Sarah — she replies within two business days") is a reasonable compromise.

**Who can answer:** Academic Registrar / whoever owns enquiry follow-up.

---

### 8. MWS-E has no voice

Both testimonials in the booklet (Kevin Otter, P3; Carolyn Bentum, P6) are **MA-EL students**. MWS-E is the cheaper, shorter, nearer door — the one most likely to convert a teacher browsing a conference table — and it currently has no quote, no photograph, and identical visual weight to the harder programme.

**What's needed:** one named MWS-E student or graduate quote. Two or three sentences is plenty; the two existing quotes are each trimmed to two.

**Who can answer:** Program Director / Advancement.

## Assets required from ICS

### 9. A portrait cover photograph — the current one prints at 156 dpi

`img/seminar-table.jpg` is 2048 × 1365 px, a 3:2 **landscape** image on a 5.75 × 8.75 in **portrait** artboard. Under `background-size: cover` the crop is height-driven: 1365 px spanning 8.75 in = **156 dpi**, or 52% of the 300 dpi print spec, on the largest and most scrutinised surface in the piece.

The plan's estimate of "2048px wide → ~355 dpi at full-bleed width" only holds for the width-driven case, which is what happens on page 2 (356 dpi, fine). Every other asset is healthy — headshots run 1,122–1,504 dpi, logos 529–581 dpi, QR codes 625–715 dpi.

**What's needed:** a portrait crop, or a ≥2625 px-tall original from the same shoot.

**Two things worth fixing in the same pass:** the current cover shows **two men**, in a piece aimed at a workforce that is roughly three-quarters women; and there is a legible third-party trademark (a Patagonia cap logo) on the subject.

---

### 10. A vector ICS logo, if one exists

Every logo in the piece is raster, max 1200 px wide. That is fine at the sizes used (529–581 dpi effective), so this is a nice-to-have rather than a defect — but if an SVG or EPS master exists anywhere, swapping it in costs nothing.

## Decisions taken, recorded so they are not re-litigated

**No number is set at display size, and that is deliberate.** The piece's argument is cost and
time, so a hero figure is tempting — but page 3 is already a seven-row fact table, and a large
number above it would compete with the block it is meant to introduce. The display hierarchy is
carried by the headlines, by the `MWS-E` / `MA-EL` program marks on the centre spread, and (since
the layout pass) by `education.icscanada.edu` at 18 pt on the back cover, which is the string a
reader actually has to retain. Revisit only if ICS wants a single figure to lead the piece.

**Page 7's unclaimed space is closed.** It was 1.06 in pooled above the QR grid. It is now
0.46 in — the widest interval on that page, which is correct, since the QR block is the only
thing on it that asks for an action. The rest went into the money block and the numbered steps.

## Questions for the printer

Not for ICS, but needed before the files go out:

1. Marks-and-bleed, or bleed-only?
2. **PDF/X-1a or plain high-quality PDF?** This one changes the build: PDF/X-1a forbids live transparency, and the piece uses two `mix-blend-mode: multiply` layers, two filter chains, and three gradients that would need flattening.
3. 8pp self-cover saddle stitch, or 4pp cover + 4pp text?
4. Confirm the CMYK build for `#2D1854` against their profile, and total ink ≤ 300%.
