Two programs, one community.

Master of Worldview Studies in Education
MWS-E

Develop Your Practice,
Enhance Your Career

For teachers, department heads, and administrators who want to think more deeply about their practice while teaching full-time.

Length
~2 years part-time
Coursework
6–7 courses
Capstone
Action Project + paper, or major research paper
Net cost
$7,000–$8,500 CAD
Starts
Three per year

Net cost assumes the $500 semester bursary. Timelines assume a steady pace; you can take longer.

An Action Project in your own school — apply your learning where you already teach, then reflect on it in a summative paper.

Two ways to finish — the Action Project, or a seventh course and a major research paper.

Begin when your year allows — entry points in Summer, Fall, and Winter.

Master of Arts (Philosophy) in Educational Leadership
MA-EL

Contribute to
the Conversation

Length
~3 years part-time
Coursework
10 courses
Capstone
Praxis & Project · in a form you choose
Net cost
~$17,000 CAD
Starts
Two per year

Net cost assumes the $500 semester bursary. Timelines assume a steady pace; you can take longer.

Four concentrations — Instructional Leadership, School Administration, Inclusion, and Literacy (the last two with Calvin University).

A capstone you shape — a curriculum, a playbook, a podcast series, a workshop, or a journal article.

A foundation for doctoral study — sustained original research under individualized faculty mentoring.
