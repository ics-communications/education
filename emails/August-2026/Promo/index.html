Courses Beginning September 2026

These online courses combine asynchronous work with six live Zoom sessions, built around the rhythms of the school year. You don't need to be a program student — every course is open to educators pursuing professional development.

 
 

MA-EL · MWS-E · Fall Term

The Craft of Reflective Practice

Dr. Gideon Strauss

This course engages educators in critical reflective practice through telling stories about their everyday working lives. Participants will learn qualitative research skills, receive an introduction to phenomenology, and develop the habits of a reflective practitioner who can examine, articulate, and improve their own professional work — moving from experience to insight.

CSTC 3 · Fall Term

Cultivating Learning Communities of Belonging

Dr. Edith van der Boom

A course for instructional leaders and administrators considering school and classroom cultures. Together we will explore how the daily behaviour of educational leaders shapes school culture, how to awaken students' knowledge, creativity, and critical reflective capacities, and how to cultivate learning communities where every student belongs.
