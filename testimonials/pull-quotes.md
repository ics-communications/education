## Carolyn Bentum
**Principal, Cairn Christian School, Stoney Creek · MA-EL student**

### Primary (themes: fits a working life, online community, calling)
> "Doing it online meant I could fit a master's program into the busyness of our life — I have four children with their own things going on. And once you break the ice, you don't even feel like you're in a Zoom room. When I was seeking out the principal role, I had messages of prayer from my classmates. After that first course, I knew this was the program that would equip me for the job I believed God was calling me to."

## Kevin Otter
**Teacher since 2009, Northumberland Christian School, Cobourg · MA-EL student**

### Primary (themes: relevance to practice, structure that flows with teaching, online community)
> "Nothing I did at ICS felt like a make-work project. Everything was either deeply relevant to my practice or deeply relevant to my interests. There's a structure for learning in the MA program that flows with a teacher's work, as busy as that life is. I'm astounded by how the ICS professors make online classes feel like a community — I miss classes."

Sources: 35:01 ("Nothing I did at ICS felt like make work projects. Everything felt either really, really relevant to my practice or really, really relevant to my interests"; "There's a structure for learning in the Ma program that flows with your work"; "I'm astounded by how the teachers at ICS can make online classes feel like a community"; "I love it, I miss it, I miss classes").

### Short alternates
> "ICS gave me the words to talk about what I do. I'm a more intentional teacher because of it." *(29:51, condensed)*

> "By the end of the course, I felt that if I bumped into anyone from my class, we'd have things to talk about for hours." *(35:01)*
